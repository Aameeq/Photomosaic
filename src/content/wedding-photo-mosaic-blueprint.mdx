What if you could see *all* of them at once?

## 1. The Power of Numbers: Why 2,000?

A great mosaic needs variety. 2,000 photos might sound like a lot, but between your professional photographer and your guests' smartphones, you likely have more than that. 

At this density, each tile is large enough to be recognizable but small enough to blend perfectly into the main portrait.

## 2. Collecting the "Hidden" Gems

Don't just use the professional shots. The best mosaics include the candid moments captured by guests.
- **Pro Tip:** Set up a shared Google Photos album or a Dropbox folder and put a QR code on the guest tables.
- **The Mix:** Aim for 30% professional shots and 70% candid guest photos for the best texture.

## 3. Choosing the "Main" Image

The hero photo should be a simple, high-contrast portrait of the couple. 
- **Avoid:** Busy backgrounds or too many small details.
- **Ideal:** A close-up or mid-shot with clear facial features. Our *Perceptual Color Logic* will handle the rest.

## 5. Printing for Posterity

For a 2,000-photo mosaic, we recommend a minimum size of **24" x 36"**. 
- **Material:** Canvas is classic, but Metal prints offer a modern, high-definition look that makes individual tiles pop.
- **Finish:** Satin or Lustre is better than high-gloss to reduce reflections on the small tiles.
