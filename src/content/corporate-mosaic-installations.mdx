## The Challenge: Scaling Detail Without Distortion
A corporate mural typically requires thousands of "tiles". The primary challenge is maintaining the integrity of these individual images while ensuring the "big picture" is instantly recognizable and color-accurate from across the lobby.

### 1. The Curation Process: Selecting the Master Image
The success of a large-format mosaic begins with the **Master Image**. For corporate environments, we typically recommend:
*   **The Brand Identity:** A high-contrast version of the company logo.
*   **The Human Connection:** A wide-angle shot of the team or a representative "hero" portrait.

Using **Perceptual Color Logic**, our AI evaluates the master image to ensure that the final selection of employee photos will align perfectly with the brand's palette.

### 2. Technical Specifications for Professional Environments
Office installations often span entire walls. In these high-visibility settings, standard digital exports are insufficient.

*   **Resolution:** Professional murals require a minimum of **300 DPI**.
*   **Format:** We provide **TIFF and PNG** exports, which preserve the lossless quality needed for museum-grade canvas or acrylic.
*   **Scale:** Our engine supports **36×48" billboard-size** renders and beyond, powered by **Cloud-Parallel Sequencing**.

## The "Living Mural": Integrating Augmented Reality
A corporate mosaic should be more than a static display—it should be an experience. By integrating **Augmented Reality (AR)**, a mural can become a portal to the company's story.

### The Interactive Service Flow:
1.  **Generate:** The AI creates the high-res mosaic.
2.  **Embed:** An AR package (powered by MindAR) is generated.
3.  **Host:** One-click publishing to **Google Drive** or manual hosting via **Netlify Drop**.
4.  **Scan:** Employees and visitors scan a discrete QR code on the mural to watch the "tiles" come to life.
